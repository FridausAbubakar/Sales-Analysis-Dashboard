# Sales Analysis Dashboards

Four dashboards built with Power BI and Excel that turn raw sales data into KPIs, trends and top/bottom performers.

## 1. Zara Sales Analysis (Power BI)
![Zara dashboard](zara-dashboard.png)
**Tools:** Power BI | **File:** Zara Sales Dashboard PBI.pbix
- 885M in total sales from 22M units across 17K products.
- Autumn is the strongest season (35% of sales); Summer is the weakest (16%).
- Promotions made little difference: sales split almost 50/50 between promoted and non-promoted items.
- Jackets lead in units sold (about 12M), well ahead of sweaters, t-shirts, shoes and jeans.

## 2. Skincare Sales Dashboard (Power BI)
![Skincare dashboard](skincare-dashboard.png)
**Tools:** Power BI | **File:** Skincare Sales Dashboard.pbix
- 20M in revenue from 500 orders and 2,504 units; average order value is 40.62K.
- Serums and cleansers each bring in about 26% of revenue; the top product is a niacinamide serum (2.2M).
- Dry skin type generates the most revenue; Acne generates the least.
- Lagos and Port Harcourt lead by region; cleansers have the highest average order value (44K).

## 3. Sales and Revenue Insights (Power BI)
![Sales and Revenue dashboard](Logistics%20Sales%20Dashboard.png)
**Tools:** Power BI
- 13M in total sales with an average order of 491.
- Sales trend upward from 2014 to 2017.
- Technology is the largest category (38%), followed by Furniture (33%).
- Phones is the top-selling sub-category.

## 4. Retail Sales Analysis (Excel)
![Excel dashboard](excel-store-dashboard.png)
**Tools:** Excel (PivotTables, slicers) | **File:** Fridaus's Dashboard.xlsx
- 545,762 in total sales and 325,059 in profit (about a 60% margin).
- Supermarkets make up 91% of sales; grocery stores make up 9%.
- Washington is the top state, followed by California and Oregon.
- Monthly sales roughly doubled in early 2019, from about 15K to about 30K.

## How to Open
Download the .pbix files and open them in Power BI Desktop.
